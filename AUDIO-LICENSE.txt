Changes: original training speech is not redistributed. New English question
scripts were synthesized with selected speaker embeddings, sentence pauses,
slower duration, peak normalization, and 64 kbps MP3 encoding. Regional labels
describe the training speaker backgrounds, not a guarantee of every regional
pronunciation. US/Canada use US phonemization; England/Australia use English RP
phonemization supported by the model. All files are synthetic speech.
Per-file speaker IDs, accents, segments and script hashes: audio-metadata.json.
